**Kestrel Run** — a valley air rally in the browser. Take off from a grass strip at dawn, thread eleven glowing rings down the Kestrel Valley, mind your fuel and your airspeed, then bring the little prop plane home onto the far runway before the light goes. Pure canvas, no libraries, no network: throttle, pitch and bank are all you have, and landing well is the whole game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — W/S throttle, arrow keys pitch and bank, G gear, F flaps, P pause, R restart, M mute. Mouse drag steers too.
